CPI measures an average basket, not your personal spending. This is gross pay before taxes, benefits or overtime premiums. Each annual salary is divided by its own paid hours and weeks; extra hours alone are not an hourly raise. The territorial options are the published capital-city CPI for Whitehorse, Yellowknife and Iqaluit, not territory-wide estimates. No daily interpolation or substitution of another province.

Where the numbers come from

Employment and Social Development Canada publishes Job Bank wage estimates through the Open Government Portal. The tool includes all occupation-region rows in the selected annual CSV, including suppressed or missing estimates. Regional labels follow the dataset's economic regions, not city boundaries.

“Edition” is the annual download release. “Reference period” is when each estimate's underlying data was measured and can be earlier. “Revision” is the source row's revision date. “Checked” is our successful retrieval, not a new wage measurement. The catalogue is checked weekly; failed downloads preserve the last validated edition and original check date.

Benefits percentage, where shown, describes employees with at least one non-wage benefit in the source data; it is not an offer guarantee. Missing local wages remain missing. Choose the provincial or national row yourself for broader context.
